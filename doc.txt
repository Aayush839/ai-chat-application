
AI Customer Support Chat Platform
Project Title

AI-Powered Customer Support Chat System

Where it is used

Industry / companies that provide services or products.

This type of system is similar to tools used by companies like

Zendesk

Intercom

These systems allow customers to chat with AI or human agents.

🎯 Project Concept

Users visit a company website and open a chat.

They can:

1️⃣ Chat with an AI support assistant
2️⃣ Chat with human support agents
3️⃣ View previous conversations

Your system will:

authenticate users (JWT)

store conversations in database

send messages in real-time

generate AI responses

maintain conversation history

🧠 Example Scenario
Customer

My order hasn’t arrived yet.

AI Assistant

Please provide your order ID so I can check the status.

Customer

ORD-2345

AI

Your order is currently in transit and will arrive tomorrow.

If AI cannot solve the issue → human agent joins chat.
